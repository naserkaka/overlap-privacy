# Overlap Privacy Policy

_Last updated November 2, 2026._

Overlap lets you share your calendar with people you choose and see when you're free together. This policy explains what information Overlap stores, who can see it, and how to delete it.

**Contact:** Naser, the developer of Overlap: naser.kakadost@hotmail.com

## What Overlap stores

| Information | Why |
|---|---|
| Your email address | To sign you in |
| Your name and chosen color | So the people you connect with recognize you |
| Your time zone and app language | To show times correctly and send notifications in your language |
| Your invite code | So friends can connect with you |
| Calendars you turn on: their names and colors, and a background photo if you choose one | To show and share them. Photos are visible only to you. |
| Events in those calendars from 7 days ago to 60 days ahead: title, location, start and end time, all-day, and whether it shows as busy or free | To show your calendar and share it at the level you choose |
| Activities you create in Overlap, and who you invite and their replies | To show them to you and the people you invite |
| Your connections and what you share with each person | To decide who sees what |
| A push notification token for each phone you use, and your notification settings | To send only the notifications you've turned on |

Overlap does **not** store calendars you leave turned off, event notes, attendees, or reminders. It never changes your phone's calendars.

Overlap does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking, and does not sell your information.

## Who can see your information

- **People you connect with** see your name and color.
- **People you share a calendar with** see that calendar at the level you choose for them:
  - **Busy only:** the times you're busy, with no titles or places. Events you've marked as "free" are not shown.
  - **Details:** titles, places, and times.
- **People you invite to an activity** see its title, time and place, and the names of other guests they're connected with.
- **Nobody else.** Sharing is per person and per calendar, and starts at nothing. You can change or stop sharing at any time; it takes effect immediately.
- Your email address and invite code are never shown to other people.

## Services Overlap uses

- **Supabase** stores Overlap's data and handles sign-in. Data is stored in the European Union (Stockholm, Sweden).
- **Sign in with Apple** and **Google Sign-In**, if you use them, confirm who you are and share your name and email address with Overlap.
- **Expo push notification service**, with **Apple Push Notification service** and **Firebase Cloud Messaging**, delivers notifications to your phone. They receive your push token and the notification text (for example, "Maya wants to connect").

## How long it's kept

- Events are refreshed from your phone and kept for the 7-day-back to 60-day-ahead window; older events are deleted at the next sync.
- Turning a calendar off deletes it and its events from Overlap's servers.
- Removing a connection stops all sharing between you and that person.
- **Deleting your account** (Settings → Delete account) permanently deletes your profile, calendars, events, activities, connections, shares, and push tokens. If you signed in with Apple, Overlap's access to your Apple ID is revoked too.

## Your choices

- Choose which calendars to upload, and what each person can see.
- Turn notifications on or off in Overlap, or in your phone's settings.
- Delete your account at any time in the app.
- To ask about your data or request a copy, email naser.kakadost@hotmail.com.

## Children

Overlap is not intended for children under 13, or under the minimum age in your country.
